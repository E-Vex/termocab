# Courses

All sixteen courses share one skeleton and one engine; what changes is each language's own grammar.

Every course has **25 stages** in five waves:

| Wave | Stages | Themes |
| --- | :---: | --- |
| Beginner | 1–5 | greetings, people and family, numbers and time, everyday verbs, describing and asking |
| Elementary | 6–10 | food, home, routine and weather, shopping and travel, yesterday and tomorrow |
| Intermediate | 11–15 | work and school, health and the body, free time and hobbies, calls and messages, stories and experiences |
| Upper-intermediate | 16–20 | city and directions, money and services, nature and the environment, opinions and news, future and dreams |
| Advanced | 21–25 | culture and the arts, science and technology, society and politics, feelings and idioms, memories and reflections |

Stage ids are append-only, so new waves can be added without disturbing anyone's saved progress.
