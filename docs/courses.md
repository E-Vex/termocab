# Courses

All thirteen courses share one skeleton and one engine; what changes is each language's own grammar.

Every course has **20 stages** in four waves:

| Wave | Stages | Themes |
| --- | :---: | --- |
| Beginner | 1–5 | greetings, people and family, numbers and time, everyday verbs, describing and asking |
| Elementary | 6–10 | food, home, routine and weather, shopping and travel, yesterday and tomorrow |
| Intermediate | 11–15 | work and school, health and the body, free time and hobbies, calls and messages, stories and experiences |
| Upper-intermediate | 16–20 | city and directions, money and services, nature and the environment, opinions and news, future and dreams |

Stage ids are append-only, so new waves can be added without disturbing anyone's saved progress.

## What each course is about

| Course | Letters and grading | Signature drills, elementary wave (6–10) | Signature drills, intermediate wave (11–15) | Signature drills, upper-intermediate wave (16–20) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **French** | the usual accents | the partitive | passé composé, *avoir mal à*, *faire du/de la*, object pronouns, imparfait | the pronouns *y*, *en* and *lui/leur*; plus/moins/aussi…que and the superlative; the futur simple |
| **Spanish** | inverted `¿` `¡` forgiven | *hay* | *tener que*, *me duele*, *estar* + gerundio, pretérito | the *tú* commands of direction; *me gustan/me encanta* agreement; *estar de acuerdo*, *parecer*; the futuro simple |
| **German** | nouns with their articles; umlauts and `ß` | modal verbs; the future bracket *werden … lesen* | Perfekt with *haben* and with *sein*, *gern*, the imperative | the two-way prepositions (*in die/in der Stadt*); *weil/dass* clauses with the verb at the end; the passive *wird/wurde*; the Präteritum |
| **Italian** | articles with their elisions; grave and acute accents | modal verbs; the passato prossimo | *dovere*, *mi fa male*, *stare* + gerundio, imperfetto | the pronouns *ci* and *ne*; *più…di/tanto…quanto*; the conditional *vorrei/potresti*; the futuro |
| **Polish** | bare nouns; every letter `ą ć ę ł ń ó ś ź ż` | *to jest*, want/like + infinitive; the *w*/*na* locative split; gendered past *-łem*/*-łam* | *mieć*, *boli mnie*, *chciałbym*, the past beyond the first person | the case-forced prepositions (*w Warszawie*, *do Krakowa*, *na dworcu*); the genitive after *potrzebować/nie mam*; the comparative *od* and the *naj-* ladders; *żeby* + past; *będę* + infinitive beside the perfective future |
| **Romanian** | the enclitic definite article blanked at the end of the noun; `ă â î ș ț` | can + infinitive; the enclitic article again | *trebuie să*, *mă doare*, *Sună-mă*, the imperfect | *până la/de la* and the articulated genitive (*în fața bisericii*); *îmi trebuie* and *am nevoie de*; the conditional *aș/ai/ar*; the *voi* future — all person-forced |
| **Portuguese** (Brazilian) | gendered articles; the *crase* `à` blanked where it lives; `ã õ ç` and the acutes and circumflexes | want/can + infinitive; Brazilian *tem* | *precisar*, the *me/te/lhe* pronouns, the pretérito against the future in a minimal pair | *ficar* for location; the service counter (*aceita cartão*, *aqui está o troco*); the conditional *gostaria/poderia*; the futuro do presente |
| **Swedish** | *en*/*ett* articles; the gender drill; the enclitic definite article; `å ä ö` | want/can + infinitive; *ska* | *måste*, *ha ont i*, the imperative, *har* + supinum | *var ligger* and *åker till*; *få* for permission and receiving (*Får jag låna*, *fick*); the *-are/-ast* ladders; *skulle vilja/kunna*; the preteritum irregulars (*var, hade, gick, såg, gav*) |
| **Hungarian** | the double acute `ő ű` folds like an accent; bare nouns; the *a*/*az* alternation; the plural suffix blanked with its linking vowel (vowel harmony made visible) | want/like/can + infinitive; homonyms *hét*, *nap*, *óra*; the *-ban*/*-ben* harmony | *mennem kell*, *fáj*, *Küldj nekem*, the *fog* future | *-ba/-be* motion against *-ban/-ben* location plus *-hoz*; *szükségem van* + *-re/-ra*; the comparative *-bb* + *mint* and *leg-*; the potential *-hat/-het*; the conditional *-nék* |
| **Turkish** | soft `ğ` folds, dotless `ı` stays closed (*hayır* is never *hayir*); the *bir* drill; the plural suffix where vowel harmony lives; *istemek* + *-mek*/*-mak* | homonyms *yemek*, *ay*, *kız*; *var*/*yok*; the four-way *-iyor* and *-dı* harmonies | *-mE lazım*, *Başım ağrıyor*, *Ara beni*, the *-ecek* future | the dative *-a/-e* with its buffers (*müzeye, bankaya, eve*); the ablative *-dan/-den*; *daha* and *en*; the necessitative *-meli/-malı*; *keşke* + *-se* |
| **Icelandic** | acutes and `ö` fold, `æ` opens as a ligature, `þ` and `ð` stay closed; the three-gender pronoun drill *hann*/*hún*/*það*; the enclitic definite article; *að* + infinitive | the *í*/*á* split; the past *-ði* with its closed `ð` | *vinna á*, *vera með*, *mig langar að*, *Hringdu í mig*, *hafa* + supine | *hvar* against *hvert*, and *fara til*; *má ég fá* and *fékk*; the *-ri/-astur* ladders; *mér finnst*; the *myndi/væri* conditional with *ef*-clauses |
| **Catalan** (as spoken in Catalonia) | the interpunct of *col·legi* folds, the hyphen *col-legi* stays a miss; gendered articles with apostrophe elisions *l'home*, *l'aigua*; all the accents | the mandatory *al*/*del* contractions; the go-past *vaig menjar* against the go-future *vaig a menjar* | *haver de*, *em fa mal*, *Truca'm*, *estar* + gerundio; also the *agradar* flip, *hi ha*, the *em/es/ens* reflexives, homonyms *temps*, *col·legi* | the pronoun *hi*; the pronoun *en*; *més…que* and *el més…de*; *hauries de*; the futur simple |
| **Dutch** (as spoken in the Netherlands) | the `ĳ` ligature (*het IJsselmeer*); the `é` of *één*, the one word where an accent does grammar; the trema `ë` | the *de*/*het* drill; the modal drill with the bare infinitive at the end; the separable verbs; *ge-* participle past against the *gaan* future in a minimal pair | *aan het* + infinitive, *hoofdpijn*, *graag*, *Bel me terug*, the simple past against the *ge-* perfect; also *hou van* with *honger* and *dorst*, *er is*/*er zijn*, *hoeveel kost/kosten*, homonyms on *gezellig* | *er* + preposition (*erheen*, *er ligt*); the separable service verbs (*Reken af*, *geld opnemen*) beside *laten*; *dan*, the ladders, *zo…als*; *zou*; *zullen* against the OVT |
